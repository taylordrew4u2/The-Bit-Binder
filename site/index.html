The notebook app for stand-up comedians

The BitBinder is a native iOS app that gives comics one place to capture jokes, brainstorm bits, build set lists, record and transcribe sets, and import old material — so your writing moves from a rough idea to a polished set without getting lost across notes apps, voice memos, and photos of notebooks.

Download on the App Store Free · iPhone & iPad · No account required

Everything a comic needs in one binder

  • Joke library

    Write or dictate jokes, group them into folders, add tags, and star your stage-ready hits. Deleted jokes go to trash so nothing is lost.

  • Brainstorm board

    Capture rough ideas as color-coded cards or voice notes, then promote the strong ones into full jokes.

  • Set lists

    Order jokes for a show, estimate runtime, add venue and date, and switch to a performance view for the stage.

  • Record & transcribe

    Record practice or live sets, keep recording as you navigate, and transcribe on-device with Apple speech recognition.

  • Roast mode

    A dedicated space for roast targets, traits, and roast jokes — kept separate from your regular material.

  • Notepad

    A single lined, scrollable notepad for freeform notes, premises, and to-dos that follows you across devices.

  • Smart import

    Bring in material from text, PDFs, photos, scanned pages, and audio. Uncertain results go to a review queue before saving.

  • BitBuddy assistant

    An on-device writing sidekick that can analyze a joke, suggest edits, and riff on premises — your material stays on your phone.

  • Private & synced

    Works offline with no account. Turn on iCloud to sync jokes, recordings, and settings across your iPhone and iPad.

How The BitBinder works

  1. Capture. Type or dictate a joke, jot a premise in Brainstorm, or scan a page from your notebook.
  2. Organize. Sort jokes into folders, tag them, and mark the ones that kill.
  3. Build a set. Drag jokes into a set list, set a target runtime, and open performance mode.
  4. Record & review. Record the set, transcribe it on-device, and compare the written joke to the live take.
  5. Keep it safe. Everything syncs to your private iCloud and can be exported whenever you want.

Frequently asked questions

What is The BitBinder?

The BitBinder is a native iOS app for stand-up comedians and comedy writers. It puts joke writing, brainstorming, set lists, audio recording, transcription, roast material, and importing in one place, so material can move from a rough idea to a polished set without getting lost.

Who is it for?

Working stand-up comedians, open-mic performers, and comedy writers who currently scatter material across notes apps, voice memos, photos of notebooks, and loose text files.

How much does it cost?

The BitBinder is free to download on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Does it work offline and keep my material private?

Yes. The app works fully offline and requires no account. Your material is stored on your device and, when you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. Transcription runs on-device with Apple speech recognition, and nothing is shared with third parties unless you explicitly export it.

Can I record and transcribe my sets?

Yes. Record practice or live sets, keep recording while you navigate the app, play recordings back, and transcribe them on-device. With iCloud on, the audio itself syncs so you can play a set on your other devices.

Can I import jokes I already wrote?

Yes. Import from plain text, PDFs, photos, scanned pages, and audio. The import pipeline extracts and splits material into individual jokes and sends uncertain results to a review queue before saving.
